# D2 · Drivers & Environmental Context

**Assessment objective**: understand the forces that necessitate or create an opportunity for modernization.

## Subdimensions and assessment criteria

| Subdimension | Assessment criteria |
|--------------|---------------------|
| D2.1 Business Drivers | Growth, expansion, transformation, efficiency, market positioning, operating model change |
| D2.2 Technology Drivers | Technology evolution, platform shifts, obsolescence, innovation, emerging architectural patterns |
| D2.3 Regulatory & Compliance Drivers | Legal, regulatory, policy, audit, standards, and compliance requirements |
| D2.4 Stakeholder Drivers | Customer expectations, workforce needs, partner requirements, and stakeholder experience |
| D2.5 Environmental Change | Competitive dynamics, market shifts, ecosystem changes, and disruptive forces |
| D2.6 Modernization Trigger | Event, constraint, threshold, initiative, failure, opportunity, or decision triggering modernization |

## Modernization signals

- Significant changes in business or operating model.
- End-of-life platforms, technologies, or architectural patterns.
- Regulatory or compliance changes.
- Increased customer expectations.
- Ecosystem shifts, acquisitions, integration needs, or competitive pressure.

## Interpretation notes

D2 is the dimension of **external and internal pressure**. Its findings rarely justify modernization alone; they weight the urgency and irreversibility of the opportunity (see [prioritization](../methodology/prioritization.md)). D2.6 (Modernization Trigger) deserves explicit capture: modernization initiated by a discrete trigger (regulatory deadline, platform end-of-life, failed initiative) behaves differently from modernization emerging from continuous pressure, and the distinction affects sequencing and staging.
