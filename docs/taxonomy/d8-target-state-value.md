# D8 · Target State & Modernization Value

**Assessment objective**: determine the desired architectural state and evaluate the modernization opportunity, options, and expected outcomes.

## Subdimensions and assessment criteria

| Subdimension | Assessment criteria |
|--------------|---------------------|
| D8.1 Target State Definition | Clarity, completeness, and viability of the desired future architecture |
| D8.2 Modernization Objectives | Specific objectives and measurable outcomes modernization is intended to achieve |
| D8.3 Solution Alternatives | Availability and evaluation of modernization alternatives and approaches |
| D8.4 Value & Benefits | Expected business, operational, strategic, financial, technical, and stakeholder value |
| D8.5 Investment & Economics | Investment requirements, costs, benefits, economics, affordability, and trade-offs |
| D8.6 Modernization Path | Sequencing, transition strategy, dependencies, incremental change, migration, and realization roadmap |

## Modernization signals

- A clear modernization opportunity exists but is not articulated.
- Modernization is proposed without a defined target state.
- Multiple options exist but have not been evaluated.
- Value, investment, and trade-offs are not understood.
- Modernization activity lacks a roadmap or measurable outcomes.

## Interpretation notes

D8 converts need into **direction**. It is the only dimension that is not purely evaluative of the present; it assesses the quality of the organization's answer to the future. Weakness in D8 does not weaken the modernization case established by D1-D6; it weakens the organization's ability to act on it, and often signals the need for target-state definition work as the first modernization intervention.

D8.6 (Modernization Path) findings, together with D7 (Readiness), determine sequencing: incremental versus staged versus transformational realization.
