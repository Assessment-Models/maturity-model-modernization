# Prioritization and Decision Logic

## No composite score as the primary outcome

The framework supports prioritization but avoids producing a single composite modernization score as its primary outcome. An average can conceal critical weaknesses and create false precision. Prioritization derives from multiple independent factors and produces **prioritization profiles** rather than relying solely on a numerical rank.

## Modernization priority factors

| Factor | What it determines |
|--------|--------------------|
| Strategic Importance | How important the architecture is to strategic execution |
| Modernization Need | Severity of current deficiencies and inadequacy |
| Future Demand | Magnitude of the gap between current fitness and future requirements |
| Exposure | Risk, economic burden, operational vulnerability, and sustainability |
| Value Potential | Expected benefit or value from modernization |
| Urgency | How soon modernization is necessary |
| Feasibility | Practical ability to execute modernization |
| Readiness | Organizational, technical, and delivery preparedness |
| Dependency | Whether modernization enables or blocks other modernization opportunities |

## Prioritization profiles

| Profile | Characterized by |
|---------|------------------|
| Critical Modernization | High need · high importance · material exposure |
| Strategic Modernization | Future demand · strategic value · capability advancement |
| Risk-Driven Modernization | Lifecycle exposure · sustainability · operational risk |
| Opportunity-Driven Modernization | Efficiency · innovation · value enhancement · simplification |

## Readiness is separate from priority

A modernization opportunity may be highly critical but difficult to execute. Such an opportunity is not simply deprioritized. It may require:

- Preparatory initiatives.
- Risk mitigation.
- Capability development.
- Staged modernization.

Readiness shapes the **path** (preparation, staging, sequencing), while priority factors establish the **need**. Qualitative anchors for each factor, profile assignment conditions, and dependency mapping (enabler/blocker relationships between opportunities) are being formalized per [ROADMAP.md](https://github.com/Assessment-Models/maturity-model-modernization/blob/main/ROADMAP.md) slice S3.
